## Navigation notes

- File encoding is **windows-1251** — open with `python3 open(..., encoding="windows-1251")` or a browser; plain `cat` will show garbage.
- The HTML line numbers above point to the chapter heading line (e.g. `IМАЛЬЧИК`). The roman numeral and title are concatenated without a space in the raw HTML.
- Each chapter body begins a few lines below its heading, after an optional subtitle line and a date line (e.g. `Воскресенье, 20 марта`).
- Chapters XLV and XLVI share one HTML line block (L432); XLVI is the second sub-section on the same page.
- Chapters XXXVIII–XXXIX are adjacent (L387, L391) with no gap — the Gorgo subplot bridges them.

## Navigation notes

- Each chapter begins with a bold paragraph number `1` in the HTML immediately after the heading line.
- To jump to chapter N, open the HTML and go to the line listed above; the heading text appears inside a `<p>` tag (not a true `<hN>` tag at the content level).
- The UA edition omits most of the Swedish provincial legends and travelogue chapters present in the full RU Brauде translation. Chapters on Lapland, provincial geography, and Dunfin/Puffin are abbreviated or absent — fall back to RU for those.
